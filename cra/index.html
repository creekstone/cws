Your Trusted Partner for CRA-Eligible Investments

Precision. Compliance. Confidence.

We are a FINRA-registered broker-dealer and CRA-specialist trading desk with decades of experience in the fixed income markets. Our sole focus is helping banks navigate the complexities of Community Reinvestment Act (CRA) investment requirements—particularly in the mortgage-backed securities space.

Our team combines deep experience in institutional bond trading with a specialized background in CRA compliance, giving us a unique ability to identify, analyze, and deliver investments that meet both credit and regulatory objectives. We blend this dual expertise with proprietary CRA data tools to deliver property-level insight into CRA-eligible investments backed by Ginnie Mae, Fannie Mae, and Freddie Mac.

We don’t just sell securities—we simplify compliance and empower investment committees with transparency, defensible research, and exam-ready documentation.

What Makes Us Different

While most CRA trading desks provide broad lists of "qualifying" CUSIPs based on ZIP codes or general issuer types, we go several levels deeper. Our platform integrates public, proprietary, and geospatial data to identify actual CRA-relevant attributes—at the property level.

We then package that research into a clear, well-documented format that your CRA committee and internal compliance teams can rely on—designed to meet regulatory expectations for documentation, performance context, and geographic targeting as outlined by OCC and FDIC CRA examination procedures.

Asset Focus

Our research and trading desk covers:

  • Ginnie Mae Project Loans
  • Fannie Mae DUS Pools and ACE Structures
  • Freddie Mac K-Deal CMBS and Multifamily PCs

We work exclusively in government-backed securities, giving you the assurance of agency credit quality combined with verifiable CRA impact.

Start Your CRA Investment Strategy Today

Our CRA trading desk is ready to help you identify and execute the right opportunities for your institution. Whether you're planning ahead for an upcoming exam or looking to deploy CRA-eligible capital, we’ll work with you to find securities that align with your regulatory goals and risk parameters.

To get started, your dedicated CRA account representative will work with you to gather the following:

  • Geographic Areas of Interest (Counties, states, MSAs, or custom assessment area overlays)
  • Targeted Investment Amount (Minimum, preferred sizing, or phased deployment timelines)
  • Products of Interest (Ginnie Mae Project Loans, Fannie Mae DUS, ACEs, Freddie Mac PCs, K-Deals)
  • Investment Parameters (Preferred durations, pricing tolerances, minimum yields, call structures)
  • Timing & Execution Needs (Quarter-end deadlines, portfolio rebalancing, exam prep timelines)

Once we understand your objectives, we’ll curate a selection of qualifying securities, deliver full CRA-backed research packages, and handle execution and post-trade support.
